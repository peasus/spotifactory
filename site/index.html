A record player for instant photos

Stand up a photo. Hear the album.

Spotifactory is a small 3D-printed easel that plays music from Instax prints of album covers. Stand a photo on it and the album starts. Lift it off and the music pauses.

Build your own See how it works

Plays through Spotify Connect. Needs Spotify Premium. Why only Spotify?

How it works

Your music, as things you can pick up and hand to someone.

  1. Print the cover

    While an album or playlist is playing, choose Print Album on the box. It prints the artwork on a paired Instax Square Link, then writes an NFC sticker for the back.

  2. Stand it up

    Set the photo in the groove. The reader behind it picks up the tag and the album starts on your speaker.

  3. Lift it to pause

    There's no pause button. Take the photo off to pause, put it back to carry on, or swap in another one to change records.

The photos

A shelf of albums you can write on.

Each print is a real Instax Square photo: a 62 mm square image on a 72 × 86 mm card, with room underneath for a note. Keep them in a crate, pin them to a board, or give one to a friend with a box of their own.

What's on the tag

A small NFC sticker on the back holds a link such as spotify:album:… or a playlist. Tags can carry more than one link, so a photo can also point to the same album on Apple Music.

Albums and playlists

Choose Print Playlist to turn a playlist into a photo the same way. Mixtapes for people who never owned a tape deck.

On the box

A little screen and a joystick. That's the whole interface.

  • Left / RightTap for the previous or next track. Hold to scrub through the song.
  • UpRepeat: off, the whole album, or one track.
  • DownShuffle on or off.
  • PressSwitch the screen between now playing, the time, and the album.
  • Side buttonsVolume up, volume down, and the menu, where you choose a speaker, print, or pair a device.

Setup

No keyboard, no app to install.

  1. Join your Wi-Fi from your phone

    On first start the box makes its own network. Connect to it and a setup page opens where you pick your Wi-Fi and type the password.

  2. Scan to sign in to Spotify

    The screen shows a QR code. Scan it, log in, and the box shows up in the Spotify app as Spotifactory.

  3. Pick where it plays

    Choose any Spotify Connect speaker from the menu, or pair a Bluetooth speaker and play from the box itself. It remembers your choice.

What's inside

Off-the-shelf parts in a 3D-printed case.

Computer
Raspberry Pi Zero 2 WRuns the player as a service and starts on power-up.
Reader
PN532 NFC readerSits behind the photo panel, under the tag.
Screen
1.3" SH1106 OLED128 × 64, with a 5-way joystick and three buttons.
Printer
Instax Square LinkPaired over Bluetooth. Shows film and battery left.
Sound
Spotify ConnectAny Connect speaker, or Bluetooth from the box. Sonos pauses when you lift the photo.
Case
3D printed in PLAAny colour you like, no supports needed. USB-C power at the back.

It keeps itself up to date. Each night at about 3:30, when nobody's listening, it checks for a new release, and rolls back on its own if the update doesn't start cleanly.

Build one

The code, the case and the parts list are all on GitHub.

Flash a Pi, run one setup script, print the case, and screw it together. The small parts import straight into a DigiKey cart, and the case model renders print-ready STL files.
