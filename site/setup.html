Set up

Get your Spotifactory playing.

About ten minutes with your phone. You join the box to your WiFi, scan two Spotify codes, then pair the Instax printer and a Bluetooth speaker. Print the one-page version.

Before you start

  • A Spotify Premium account. Why only Spotify?
  • Your WiFi name and password. The box uses 2.4 GHz WiFi, which almost every home router has.
  • A phone with a camera, on the same WiFi you want the box on.
  • The Instax Link printer, charged and with film in it, and a Bluetooth speaker, charged.

The buttons

  • JoystickUp and down move through a menu. Press it in to choose. Left goes back.
  • Top buttonVolume up.
  • Middle buttonVolume down.
  • Bottom buttonMenu. Press it again to close the menu.

On any screen with a QR code, push the joystick right to see the same thing in words, and right again to go back to the code.

1. Join your WiFi

  1. Plug the box in. After the logo (up to half a minute), it shows Join WiFi Spotifactory for setup. The box has made its own WiFi network for setting up.

  2. Point your phone's camera at the QR code on the screen and tap Join. Or open your phone's WiFi settings and pick Spotifactory. It has no password.

  3. A setup page opens by itself. If it doesn't, open your browser and go to 10.42.0.1.

  4. Pick your home WiFi, type its password, and tap Connect.

  5. The Spotifactory network disappears and your phone goes back to your own WiFi. The box shows Joining WiFi... and then WiFi connected!

Wrong password? The box says Couldn't join and the Spotifactory network comes back within a minute. Join it again and the page tells you what went wrong, so you can try again.

2. Connect Spotify (first code)

This code lets the box see what's playing, start albums from your photos, and pick speakers.

  1. Once the WiFi is connected, the box shows a QR code with Scan Code next to it.

  2. Scan it with your phone's camera and open the link.

  3. Log in to Spotify with the account you'll use on the box, and tap Agree.

  4. The page says Connected! and the box says Spotify connected! Then it moves straight on to the second code.

No camera handy? Push right to see a short web address you can type on any phone or computer instead.

3. Let the box play (second code)

This code signs in the box's own player, so music plays from the box even when your phone can't see it on the WiFi. Use the same Spotify account as the first code.

  1. The box shows Scan so box can play and a short code, such as K7QW2M.

  2. Scan it with your phone. It opens spotify.com/pair with the code filled in. Or go to spotify.com/pair on any device and type the code.

  3. Log in if Spotify asks, and approve.

  4. The box says Box can play now! and goes to its home screen.

Each code lasts a few minutes. If it runs out, the box shows a new one by itself.

4. Pair the printer

  1. Turn the Instax printer on. If the Instax app on your phone is open, close it so the phone lets go of the printer.

  2. Press the Menu button, choose Bluetooth, then + Pair new device.

  3. The box looks for devices for about 10 seconds. Choose the one called INSTAX- followed by numbers.

  4. The box says Printer ready!

To check, choose Film Left from the menu. It shows how many shots are left and the printer's battery.

5. Pair a Bluetooth speaker

  1. Put the speaker in pairing mode. On most speakers you hold the Bluetooth button until the light flashes.

  2. Press Menu, choose Bluetooth, then + Pair new device.

  3. Choose your speaker from the list. The box says Paired! and the speaker's name, and music plays through it from then on.

Whenever you turn the speaker on, the box reconnects to it by itself within about half a minute. To switch to another paired speaker, go to Menu › Bluetooth, choose it, then Use as speaker.

Speakers on your WiFi don't need pairing. See supported speakers below.

Supported speakers

Bluetooth is the easy way: pair once (step 5) and you're done. If your speakers are on your WiFi instead, pick them from Menu › Choose Speaker, no pairing needed.

  • Bluetooth speakers

    Paired with the box. Reconnects by itself when the speaker turns on.

    Tested

  • Spotify Connect

    Any speaker, TV or soundbar that shows up as a device in the Spotify app.

    Tested

  • Sonos

    Over WiFi through Spotify Connect. A Move or Roam can pair over Bluetooth once it's in Bluetooth mode.

    Tested

  • Chromecast and Google Cast

    Nest speakers, Chromecast Audio and Cast speaker groups, on your WiFi.

    New, being tested

  • AirPlay

    HomePod and other AirPlay 2 speakers on your WiFi. Ones that need a PIN are left out.

    New, being tested

  • Music Assistant

    For people who already run a Music Assistant server.

    Advanced, untested

The box plays to one speaker at a time; a Cast group counts as one. Some mesh WiFi systems (Google Wifi, for one) can hide Cast and AirPlay speakers from the box. Bluetooth always works.

Your first photo

  1. Play an album in the Spotify app and pick Spotifactory as the device.

  2. Press Menu, choose Print Album, then Yes. The cover prints.

  3. Stick an NFC tag on the back of the photo. When the box says Place tag to write, stand the photo on the box. It says Album ready!

  4. From then on, stand that photo on the box to play the album.

If something goes wrong

  • The setup page didn't open. Stay on the Spotifactory WiFi and go to 10.42.0.1 in your browser.
  • New WiFi, or you moved house. When the box can't find its saved WiFi it opens WiFi setup by itself. You can also start it any time from Menu › WiFi Setup.
  • The box says "Can't reach login". Check the WiFi is working, then choose Menu › Connect Spotify to try again.
  • Spotify won't let you log in to the box. If someone else built it for you, ask them to add your Spotify email to their Spotify app (see below).
  • The printer isn't in the list. It turns itself off after a while. Press its power button and choose + Pair new device again.
  • "Printer Off?" when printing. Turn the printer on and choose Retry.
  • Updates install by themselves overnight. To get them now, choose Menu › Check for Updates.

Giving one as a gift

If you built the box and are giving it away, do these before it leaves your house:

  1. Add the new owner's Spotify email under User Management in your Spotify developer app (step 3 of the build). Spotify allows five people per app.

  2. Choose Menu › Check for Updates, so it starts out on the newest version.

  3. Print the one-page setup sheet and put it in the box.
